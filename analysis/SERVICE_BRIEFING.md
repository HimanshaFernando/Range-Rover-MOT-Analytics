## Workshop scenario

For 200 future initial inspections with the same mix, the observed tyres rate implies about **10 affected inspections**. A single vehicle can appear in several categories. This scenario assumes stable rates and fleet mix. Service bookings, repair labour, repair prices and parts consumption require separate data.

## Interpretation

The outcome describes condition at the MOT test. Age and mileage adjustment addresses two measured differences. Vehicle use, prior maintenance and local testing practices can also influence the comparison. Model names are rule-mapped from tester records. See data/model_mapping.csv for the exact labels and docs/METHODS.md for eligibility.
